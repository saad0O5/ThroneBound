## Notes for your team's design pass
- Numbers above are a first draft — expect to tune Attack/Health/cost once you start playtesting matches
- "Pack" (Beastkin), spell-chaining (Arcane), and sacrifice/death-trigger (Undead) are each faction's core identity — keep new card ideas aligned to these themes so factions stay distinct
- A few cards ("Death Knight", "Necromancer") reference other cards existing in specific zones (graveyard, board) — these are good candidates to prototype first since they'll validate your `GameState` design early
